In Germany, are you more likely to be in an accident on a Damp/Wet Road or an Slippery/Snow Road?

Human intuition suggests that icy roads are more dangerous, because ice feels dramatically more dangerous and slippery. People tend to focus on the perceived risk rather than the actual data.
And, data tells a different story.

Based on severity, on which type of roads do most accidents occur?

On which road conditions do most of accidents occurs, based on severity?

Most of the accidents occur on dry roads. More people tend to drive their vehicles on dry roads, espeically during summer season, which leads to higher traffic volume and consequently more accidents. Additionally, drivers may be less cautious on dry roads, leading to more accidents.

On the other hand, contrary to what one might expect, even though icy and snowy roads are more dangerous, they typically see fewer accidents. This might because of the "Human Tendency" - Fewer people drive in those conditions and those who do are often more cautious.

Wet/Damp roads have a higher count of accidents compared to icy/snowy roads.

  • This could be because people may underestimate the risks associated with them, leading to more accidents.
  • Additionally, wet roads can cause hydroplaning, which can lead to loss of control and accidents.

Wait !!!, what is that spike in 2021?

Do you see the spike for Slippery/Snowy road conditions in the year 2021?

Lets take a closer look.

In 2021, there was spike in the number of accidents on slippery/snowy road conditions.

  • For accident leading to fatality, there was a 3x increase.
  • We see a similar trend for seriously injured accidents, an increase of 134%
  • In terms of absoluted increase, non-serious accidents also saw a notable rise, from 2231 to 5945

This is most likely the Dip & Rebound Effect, a result of the relaxation of lockdown measures caused due to COVID-19 pandemic in 2020.

A increase is expected, but not such a huge spike.

Furthermore, we dont see similar spikes for the other road conditions, only for slippery/snowy roads.

Perhaps a possible reason could be a sudden and strong cold/snow event in February 2021, bringing heavy snowfall up to around 30–80 cm locally, strong winds, and traffic chaos, which led to many accidents and disruptions. Source

A Wet/Damp road is perhaps more dangerous than one might think.
Statistically, you are more likely to be in an accident while travelling on a damp/wet road compared to travelling on a slippery/snowy road.

It is as essential to drive with extra caution on wet/damp roads as well as on slippery/snowy roads.
